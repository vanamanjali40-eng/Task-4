Suggested Power BI DAX Measures

Total Sales = SUM(sales_financial_dataset[Sales])

Total Profit = SUM(sales_financial_dataset[Profit])

Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(sales_financial_dataset[Order_ID])

Units Sold = SUM(sales_financial_dataset[Units_Sold])

Profit Margin = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)

Sales Growth % =
VAR CurrentSales = [Total Sales]
VAR PreviousSales =
    CALCULATE([Total Sales], DATEADD(sales_financial_dataset[Order_Date], -1, MONTH))
RETURN
    DIVIDE(CurrentSales - PreviousSales, PreviousSales, 0)

Tip: In Power BI, set Order_Date to Date data type and create/use a proper Calendar table for robust time intelligence.
